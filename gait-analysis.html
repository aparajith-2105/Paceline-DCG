Book gait analysis

A free run assessment every Saturday. Leave knowing which shoes suit the way you run.

What to expect

  1. 1

    Chat

    Tell us how often you run, what you wear now and any niggles you’ve had.

  2. 2

    Run

    A few minutes on our treadmill, filmed from behind and the side, so we can see how your foot lands.

  3. 3

    Try

    We go through the footage with you, then bring out pairs that suit your stride to try on.

What to bring

  • Your current running shoes, even if they’re worn out
  • Running shorts or leggings you can move in
  • Any notes on injuries or pain
